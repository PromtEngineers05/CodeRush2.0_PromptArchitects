# 🏙️ CivicAI
### AI-Powered Civic Intelligence & Public Safety Platform

**Transforming Cities from Reactive to Predictive Governance**

# 📖 Overview

CivicAI is an AI-powered civic operating system designed to help citizens, government officers, and city administrators manage urban infrastructure more intelligently.

Instead of being just another complaint portal, CivicAI combines Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Geospatial Intelligence, Predictive Analytics, and Public Safety into one unified platform.

The platform enables cities to detect civic issues, predict infrastructure failures, improve government response, enhance public safety, and support data-driven governance.

# 🚀 Problem Statement

Urban governance today is mostly reactive.
Citizens report issues manually.
Departments work independently.
Infrastructure maintenance is delayed.
Decision making lacks real-time intelligence.
Public safety systems are disconnected.
Cities require a unified AI platform capable of assisting both citizens and government agencies.
